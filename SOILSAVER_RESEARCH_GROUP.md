## What SoilSaveR is now

SoilSaveR is presented as a **Research Group**, not just a project. It builds the
first open-access, multi-modal, multi-region dataset for erosion & land
degradation detection. The group has won a **NASA Space-to-Soil prize challenge
award** with its solution **SoilSentinel** (a related but separate programme —
never conflate SoilSaveR with SoilSentinel).
